The practical details

A plan, not a lock-in.

Start with the work you have today.
The plan can change when the work does.

01Your workspaceCan I mix plans in one workspace?

Each workspace has one plan. Everyone with a paid seat gets the same features, while invited guests can work in the projects you share with them.

02Your billWhat changes if I choose annual billing?

Studio and Team cost 20% less per person. You pay for a year at a time; automation allowances still renew each month. Enterprise pricing is agreed separately.

03A change of plansWhat happens if our team gets smaller?

You can remove seats or change plans. Changes take effect at the next renewal, and you can export your work at any time.

04This demoCan I actually sign up for Tandem?

Tandem is a fictional service, built to demonstrate a real comparison page with Prastora. There are no accounts, charges or awkward sales follow-ups.

A clearer route to delivery

Connect tasks across projects. If a deadline moves, dependent dates are flagged for review, never silently rescheduled.

Room for a second opinion

Free includes two guests. Guests can comment on invited projects but cannot see the rest of your workspace.

Nothing useful disappears overnight

Restore any document revision from the last 90 days. Deleted documents stay recoverable for 30 days.

10,000 runs, shared by your team

One completed rule counts as one run. The allowance is shared across your workspace and resets each month, even with annual billing.

Bring your own good ideas

Read and update projects through the API. Webhooks send task and project events to your own services. Rate limits apply to every plan.

See capacity before saying yes

Compare assigned work with each person’s working hours. Planned leave is taken into account; a busy calendar is not a performance score.

Space for the working files

The 1 TB allowance belongs to the workspace, not each person. A single upload can be up to 5 GB.

One way in, fewer loose ends

Connect your identity provider with SAML SSO and provision or remove members with SCIM. Setup is included in onboarding.

A person who knows your workspace

Your named support partner helps with rollout, migration and ongoing administration. Response targets are agreed before you sign.
