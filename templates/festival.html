Arriving from other stories

Wyld Stallyns · Duke Silver · Sex Bob-Omb · The Soggy Bottom Boys · The Blues Brothers

A different kind
of connection.

For one day, an old railway station welcomes musicians from stories that never quite shared a world. There are loud guitars in the Yard, close harmonies beneath the canopy, and a saxophone in the Waiting Room.

Come for a familiar song. Stay for something you missed the first time. All three stages are a few minutes’ walk apart.

A few introductions,
in case it has been a while.

01

Rock across time · Bill & Ted

Wyld Stallyns

Bill S. Preston and Ted Theodore Logan are two friends from San Dimas with big guitars and an unusually complicated rehearsal schedule. Wyld Stallyns bring people together with a song that reaches across time.

Bill and Ted close the festival. Big guitars, a generous finale, and an audience that has come a little further than usual.

Learn more ↗Bill & Ted’s Excellent Adventure on Apple TV ↗
02

After-hours jazz · Parks and Recreation

Duke Silver

Duke Silver is a saxophonist with a devoted following and a quiet life outside the spotlight. By day, he answers to Ron Swanson. By night, his warm, unhurried jazz makes a small room feel like the only place to be.

Warm saxophone, a small room, and a good reason to leave an hour unplanned. Find Duke in the Waiting Room; you are welcome to stay.

Learn more ↗Parks and Recreation on Peacock ↗
03

Garage rock · Scott Pilgrim vs. the World

Sex Bob-Omb

Scott Pilgrim, Kim Pine and Stephen Stills make up this Toronto garage band. Their songs are short, rough around the edges and played with conviction. A modest rehearsal space has never stopped them from making a considerable amount of noise.

Short songs, loud amplifiers, and the energy of a rehearsal room with the door left open. They take over the Yard twice. Earplugs are a sound investment.

Learn more ↗Scott Pilgrim vs. the World on Apple TV ↗
04

Folk & bluegrass · O Brother, Where Art Thou?

The Soggy Bottom Boys

Everett, Pete, Delmar and guitarist Tommy Johnson became a recording sensation almost by accident. Close harmonies, a wandering spirit and I Am a Man of Constant Sorrow have carried the Soggy Bottom Boys further than they expected.

Close harmonies and songs that travel well. Their first set starts beneath the old platform canopy, with a shorter return as the light begins to change.

Learn more ↗O Brother, Where Art Thou? on Apple TV ↗
05

Soul / rhythm & blues · The Blues Brothers

The Blues Brothers

Jake and Elwood Blues put their band back together to save the orphanage where they grew up. With a formidable rhythm section, a full set of horns and an unwavering sense of purpose, the brothers know how to get a room on its feet.

The evening opens with a full horn section and enough rhythm to fill the old railway yard. We have left plenty of room in front of the stage.

A few things
before you arrive.

Is this a real festival?

No. OFFSCREEN is a fictional festival on 31 August 2100, made to explore layouts with Prastora. There are no tickets to buy or journeys to book.

What happens when two sets overlap?

You get to choose. The programme shows the full day on a large screen and a chronological list on a small one. Use the stage filters to find your route.

Where can I see the original films and series?

Each artist introduction includes a link to an official viewing service. Check its availability in your country; a programme from 2100 cannot promise today’s catalogue.
