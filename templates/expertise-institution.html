European Legal Expertise Association

Legal expertise for matters that cross borders

ELEA brings together independent lawyers and legal experts across Europe and beyond. We assemble the right expertise for each matter, coordinate work across jurisdictions and languages, and deliver it through a shared professional framework.

Areas of practice

ELEA's priority practice areas

These are the fields in which the Association actively develops its expertise, publications and client services. Members also offer services in further areas of their own practice.

  • Cross-border estates

    Property, bank accounts and shareholdings abroad, foreign wills and beneficiaries in different countries.

  • Trusts and family foundations

    Establishment, governance, recognition across jurisdictions and the rights of beneficiaries.

  • Business and assets

    Foreign shareholdings, corporate disputes, ownership structures and cross-border assets.

  • Foreign law before courts

    Independent opinions on foreign law for courts, tribunals, notaries and public authorities.

  • Recognition and enforcement

    Enforcing judgments and arbitral awards where debtors or assets are located.

  • Members' specialist practices

    Residence and relocation, family law, banking compliance, cryptoassets, sanctions, extradition and international protection.

One matter, several legal systems

A cross-border matter in practice

Consider an estate involving a person who lived in Germany, a flat in Berlin, a villa in Cyprus, an apartment in Dubai, a shareholding in Poland and investments held by a Lithuanian bank. The will was made in Ukraine. The surviving family live in Germany and Canada, and the will is contested.

Professional standards

A shared approach to legal expert work

Members undertake to follow ELEA's Code of Practice. Individual experts reach and sign their own professional conclusions; the Association organises delivery and checks work against the procedures applicable to each engagement.
