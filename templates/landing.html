How a case gets solved

  1. The call

    Tell us what happened. All of it, including the part where someone deployed on a Friday afternoon.

  2. The legwork

    We follow the trail through logs, traces and commit history. Every witness gets questioned, the intern's cron job included.

  3. The report

    A plain account of what broke, why it broke and what keeps it from breaking again. We check DNS first. It's always DNS – until it isn't.

Questions people ask the detective

Why a sloth?

Fast is how you got into this. We look carefully, change one thing at a time and leave the place tidier than we found it.

How slow is slow?

Most cases close within a day. Slow is about method, not about your pager going off for a week.

Do you need access to production?

Read-only, and only to what you allow. The detective doesn't pick locks. Usually.

Can we blame the intern?

You can. The report will still show who approved the pull request.

Is Slowburn real?

No. It's a demo template for Prastora, a CSS framework. The sloth abides, though.
