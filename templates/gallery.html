Port Ellery before breakfast

Between the first tram and the first coffee, the city belongs to gulls, fishmongers and one very determined bakery. Seven pictures from a single morning.

Photographs by Inês Moreira · Words by Marta Quill · Tap any picture to see it larger

At six the city sounds like a kettle: the tram on the bridge, the gulls arguing about lamp posts, somebody's radio on the fish quay. By eight it sounds like everywhere else.
