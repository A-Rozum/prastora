Start with the page structure
How do the proportions change?

Desktop uses four grid columns, with two artworks spanning a pair of columns. Mobile uses two columns and gives the wider artworks their own row. The source order stays the same.

Can I replace these with photographs?

Yes. Use images with useful alternative text and appropriate dimensions. For larger photographs, provide responsive sources and load below-the-fold media lazily where appropriate.

What happens when a caption is longer?

It finds its natural height. Titles and descriptions are not truncated, and destinations remain visible without hovering over an image.
