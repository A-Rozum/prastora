Compose around the content.

Sections, stacks and grids give you a small set of reusable decisions. Use a wide introduction, pair detailed content with an aside, or bring several cards into the same row.

  • Use a section to establish the page's outer spacing.
  • Use a stack for a predictable rhythm between headings, paragraphs and actions.
  • Use a grid or pair when the content benefits from a shared composition.
A layout pattern should help you organise the page, not decide what your site is allowed to contain.

Start with HTML. Refine with CSS.

  1. Choose an example. Begin with the composition closest to the page you need.
  2. Replace the content. Let headings and paragraphs find their natural height rather than forcing them into fixed boxes.
  3. Adjust the theme. Change colours, spacing and typography through CSS variables.
  4. Review on real screens. Check reading, navigation and longer translations on the devices your visitors use.

Common questions

Do I need a build tool?

Not for these examples. They use ordinary HTML, CSS and a local SVG sprite. Build-time templates can be added later without turning the site into a client-side application.

Does Prastora replace responsive breakpoints?

No. Proportional scaling and responsive composition do different jobs. A grid can become a single reading column while its typography continues to follow the chosen scale.

Can I change the design without changing the structure?

The core primitives and demonstration theme are separate. You can replace the theme or extend individual components without rebuilding every page.
