Why it exists

Phones take good photos. They also take calls, messages, news alerts and forty minutes of your evening. The Crumb One does one thing: it sits in a jacket pocket until something on the table deserves better than a quick snap.

The sensor is big enough for candlelit dinners, the lens is sharp enough to count the sesame seeds, and the controls are where your thumbs expect them. No feeds, no notifications, no opinions about your screen time.

  • Fits in a jacket pocket. Fits in a jeans pocket if you're optimistic.
  • Low-light sensor that rescues dinners lit by one sad candle.
  • About 400 shots per charge, or one very long brunch.
