The dash in every text produced in the system is U+2013 (–): text an agent generates, writes or edits, in repositories, on sites and in replies. Quoted and source material keeps its own characters: quotations in typographic marks («…», “…”, „…“), block quotations, code, and files a project lists under `verbatim:` in typography.yaml at its root. A project whose owner chose another dash says so in its AGENTS.md and in typography.yaml (`dash: em`); the tools then leave its texts as written. The tools set the dash at every commit made in an agent's environment and at every landing.
