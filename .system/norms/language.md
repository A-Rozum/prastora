Text kept in Plyń repositories (plyn-*) is in English, whatever language the agent and the user converse in: service texts, specifications, drafts, workshop entries, discussion records and commit messages. The owner of a project sets the language of its repositories; English is the default. Material keeps its own language where the language is part of it: sources and quotations, client documents, translations, glossaries, and deliverables requested in another language.
