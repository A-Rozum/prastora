Service texts (README, AGENTS.md, instructions, element texts) state what to do and what is needed to do it, only where it differs from what an agent already does reliably; a prohibition only where the prohibited action would otherwise be the default. Describe purposes, capabilities, responsibilities and boundaries affirmatively. Use contrastive negation where it clarifies a likely misunderstanding or a real boundary more precisely than an affirmative formulation. Replace open-ended lists of what something is not with a precise account of what it is. Keep discussion history, check dates, reasoning and facts obtainable in one command out of service texts.
