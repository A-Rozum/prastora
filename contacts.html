Keep feedback beside the field.

Static error and success states use words as well as colour. These samples do not validate edits or verify ownership of an address.

Help visitors before they need to ask.

Can I use this as a contact-page starting point?

Yes. Adapt the labels, fields and destinations to your site. Form processing and any required privacy information belong to your implementation.

Does the page need an embedded map?

Only if a location helps the visitor. The Google map here loads lazily as you approach it; loading can send data to Google. The regional example uses local SVG geography and native city links without JavaScript or remote map tiles.
