Can I use this as a contact-page starting point?

Yes. Adapt the labels, fields and destinations to your site. Form processing and any required privacy information belong to your implementation.

Does the page need an embedded map?

Only if a physical location helps the visitor. A useful address or directions link may be enough; this example does not invent an office or load a map service.
