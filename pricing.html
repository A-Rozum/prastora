Keep the fine print readable

Answer the questions that affect the choice.

Put important limits next to the options. Use disclosures for supporting detail, not to hide the difference between packages.

Explore the contact page
Are these prices for Prastora?

No. The figures are illustrative content for this pricing-page pattern. Prastora does not offer these packages or a connected checkout.

Can the cards contain different amounts of text?

Yes. Each card follows its content while the action area remains at the bottom of the shared row. On mobile, cards form a single reading column.

Can I use a different currency or billing period?

Replace the amounts and labels with your own terms. Keep the currency, billing period and any material conditions visible near the price.

What needs connecting on a live site?

Purchasing, payment, account access and form processing need their own integrations. This page demonstrates their composition, not those services.
